Ledgr is a privacy-first, local-first personal finance and budget tracking app built with Flutter.

Key features:
• Complete privacy: All financial data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database via Drift. No account, no cloud lock-in, and zero trackers or analytics.
• Multi-wallet tracking: Manage cash, checking, savings, credit cards, loans, and investment accounts in one place with multi-currency support and live exchange rates.
• Smart budgeting: Set monthly, periodic, or category-specific spending limits and monitor progress with real-time indicators.
• Cash flow forecasting: Project future wallet balances 30, 60, or 90 days ahead based on scheduled bills, recurring subscriptions, and income.
• Investment portfolio: Track stocks, ETFs, and crypto holdings with cost basis, current valuations, and asset allocation breakdown.
• Net worth tracking: Monitor assets minus liabilities over time with historical snapshots and trends.
• App security: Protect your financial records with biometric authentication (fingerprint / Face Unlock) or secure PBKDF2-hashed PIN.
• Local backups & CSV: Export and restore full encrypted backups, or import and export transactions via CSV.
• Clean Material 3 design: Supports light, dark, and true AMOLED dark modes with customizable theme accents.
