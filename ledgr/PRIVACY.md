# Ledgr Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-12

Ledgr is designed as a local-first personal finance app.

## Data stored by the app

Ledgr may store the financial information you enter, including wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring payments, objectives, portfolio holdings, net-worth snapshots, settings, and backup/export preferences.

This data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or import files using platform-provided file and sharing tools.

## Network access

Ledgr does not require an account or hosted Ledgr backend for core usage. Network access may be used for features that need external information, such as exchange-rate lookups. Requests to third-party services are subject to those services' own privacy policies.

## Authentication and app lock

When app lock is enabled, Ledgr uses platform authentication APIs such as biometric or device credential prompts. Ledgr does not receive or store biometric templates.

## Notifications

If enabled, Ledgr may schedule local notifications on your device. Notification data is handled by the operating system notification service.

## Backups, exports, and sharing

When you create backups, export CSV files, or share files, you control where those files are saved or sent. Those files may contain sensitive financial data. Store and transmit them carefully.

## Data deletion

You can delete data inside the app where supported, uninstall the app, or remove exported backup files from locations where you saved them.

## Contact

For privacy questions or security reports, open an issue in the public repository or contact the project maintainers through the repository's listed contact channels.
